Help

Deaf → hearing: tap signs on the Sign keyboard or use Camera mode. Audii builds the text and can speak it.

Hearing → deaf: type a message, or press Voice mode and speak. Audii writes what you said and shows it as ASL fingerspelling.

Voice mode uses your browser’s speech recognition (best in Chrome, Edge and Safari), which may send audio to the browser vendor. Audii uses the American manual alphabet; other sign languages use different handshapes.
