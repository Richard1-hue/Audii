About Audii

A practical prototype designed to make deaf/hearing communication more accessible.

1. BuildUse the alphabet board or type Audii symbols.
2. TranslateAudii converts recognized symbols into ordinary alphabet text.
3. SpeakYour browser's speech engine reads the resulting message aloud.
4. ExtendA future version can connect camera-based sign-language recognition to this interface.
Prototype note: sign languages are real languages, and hand shapes differ by language and region. This version uses a clear symbol layer rather than claiming to recognize a camera image as real sign language.
